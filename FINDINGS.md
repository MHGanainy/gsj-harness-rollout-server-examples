| F-08 | **BLOCKER-class numerics** (worked around) | Polar's vendored `slime_bridge/reward_post_process.py` explodes the advantage scale on sparse binary reward. Its leave-one-out scale is `statistics.stdev(other_means) + 1e-6`; when every *other* trajectory carries the same reward — the common case at demo scale, e.g. 1 rewarded episode in 27 — `stdev == 0` exactly and the scale collapses to the `1e-6` epsilon, so that trajectory's advantage becomes `(1.0 − 0.0)/1e-6 = 1e6`. Measured live at CP-17: `rollout/advantages` mean **37036.85** against `raw_reward` 0.037, `train/loss −37026.5`, `train/grad_norm 450053`. Megatron's `clip_grad 1.0` bounded the actual update, so this degrades to "one outlier dominates the step direction" rather than diverging — but nothing warns. Cure applied: `--disable-grpo-std-normalization` (Dr.GRPO), which yields `grad_norm 0.4513` and LOO-centered advantages summing to ~0. The principled fix is a degenerate-variance guard (skip normalization, or zero the advantage, when `stdev ≈ 0`) rather than an epsilon floor | no — Polar-side (the vendored post-processor) | OPEN, worked around + both runs recorded |
| F-09 | DOC (the F-08 question, asked of verl) | verl @ `1ae9455` does NOT have the F-08 shape, despite textually identical `(score − mean)/(std + 1e-6)` in `compute_grpo_outcome_advantage` (`core_algos.py:326`): its std is over the WHOLE uid group, self included, so `std == 0` implies every deviation is exactly 0 — the 1/ε factor only ever multiplies zero. With the CP-17 distribution (1 rewarded in 27, one group) max abs advantage is **≈5.0**, not 1e6; with all-identical rewards the advantages are exactly 0. verl's actual leave-one-out estimator (`compute_rloo_outcome_advantage`, `core_algos.py:588`) centres by the LOO mean and never divides by a variance (its `epsilon` parameter is dead code), so the F-08 mechanism has no counterpart to fire. Both behaviours pinned as executable tests (`test_f08_shape_verl_grpo_is_structurally_immune`, `test_verl_rloo_has_no_variance_division`) | no — verl-side, and it's the good kind | CLOSED at CP-20 (measured, test-pinned) |
