| F-08 | **BLOCKER-class numerics** (worked around) | Polar's vendored `slime_bridge/reward_post_process.py` explodes the advantage scale on sparse binary reward. Cure applied: `--disable-grpo-std-normalization` (Dr.GRPO), which yields `grad_norm 0.4513` and LOO-centered advantages summing to ~0. The principled fix is a degenerate-variance guard (skip normalization, or zero the advantage, when `stdev ≈ 0`) rather than an epsilon floor | no — Polar-side (the vendored post-processor) | OPEN, worked around + both runs recorded |
